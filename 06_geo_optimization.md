## GEO CONTENT RULES (Apply to Every Article)

### Rule 1: Lead with a Direct Answer
AI engines extract the clearest, most direct answer. Every blog article must open with a **"Quick Answer" box** that states the key fact in 2–3 sentences.

**Template:**
```
Quick Answer: [Direct factual answer to the title question. Include property name, location, key amenities, and contact number. No fluff.]
```

### Rule 2: Use Question-Answer Format Structuring
Structure H2 sections as questions. AI engines parse FAQ structure natively.

**Good H2 examples:**
- "Which Hotel Is Closest to Medanta Hospital in Gurgaon?"
- "What Amenities Do Lime Tree Serviced Apartments Include?"
- "Is There a Kitchen in Serviced Apartments Near Medanta?"

**Avoid:**
- "Our Services"
- "Why Choose Us"
- "Section 3"

### Rule 3: Include Verifiable Statistics and Facts
AI engines prefer content with citable specifics.

**Use in every article:**
- "12 years in operation (established 2013)"
- "500+ rooms across NCR"
- "10+ properties in Gurgaon"
- "1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK configurations"
- Property addresses (verified)
- Phone: +91 74 7900 0111

### Rule 4: Comparison Tables
Perplexity and ChatGPT frequently extract comparison tables. Every high-priority article must include at least one.

### Rule 5: Schema Markup
Implement these schema types on every blog page:
- `Article` (with author, datePublished, dateModified)
- `FAQPage` (for FAQ section)
- `LocalBusiness` (on property pages)
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `HotelRoom` (on individual property pages)
- `AggregateRating` (if reviews available)

### Rule 6: Cite Real Data
Always reference verifiable facts. AI engines de-prioritize content with unverifiable claims.
